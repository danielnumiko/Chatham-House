Keynote speakers

Event chaired by James Harding.

  • Dr Simon Smith

    Founding Partner, Horizon Insights

    Former Permanent Under-Secretary at the Foreign Office, Simon now advises governments and businesses on regulatory and political risk in international markets. During his diplomatic career he ran the UK’s global network of embassies and sat on the National Security Council. He is a founding partner of Horizon Insights and a visiting professor in international relations.

  • Professor Casey Brown

    Associate Fellow, Asia-Pacific Programme

    Leads Chatham House research on competition policy in East Asia and has advised the OECD on cross-border merger review. Casey’s recent work examines how China’s anti-monopoly regime shapes global deal-making, and she is a regular commentator for international broadcasters. Before joining Chatham House she spent eight years in private practice in Hong Kong and Singapore.

    Read more about Professor Casey Brown

  • Jordan Jones

    Chief Economist, Strategic Horizons

    Jordan spent a decade at the European Commission’s competition directorate and now writes on how digital markets are reshaping antitrust enforcement. At Strategic Horizons, Jordan leads economic analysis for clients facing merger reviews in the EU and UK, and has published widely on platform regulation, data access and interoperability.

  • Professor Alexandra Montgomery-Whitfield

    Director of Competition and Markets Policy, Institute for Regulatory Studies

    An expert in merger control and state aid, Alexandra has given evidence to parliamentary committees in the UK, Canada and Australia. She directs the Institute for Regulatory Studies’ competition and markets programme, advises several national regulators on enforcement strategy, and is the author of a widely used textbook on European competition law.

  • Dr Morgan Chase

    Partner, Insight Partners

    Morgan advises technology firms on competition investigations and previously led the digital markets team at a national regulator. Morgan’s practice focuses on cloud services, app stores and AI partnerships, and Morgan sits on the advisory board of a leading competition law journal.

  • Professor Taylor Ash

    Senior Research Fellow, Global Economy and Finance Programme

    Taylor researches the geopolitics of industrial policy and previously served as an adviser at HM Treasury. Taylor’s current project examines how subsidy races between the US, EU and China are reshaping supply chains, and Taylor co-chairs the Chatham House working group on economic security.
